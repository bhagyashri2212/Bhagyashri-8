History of Harihar Fort

Harihar Fort, also known as Harshgad, was constructed during the Yadava dynasty. It was strategically important due to its location overlooking the trade route connecting Nashik to Gujarat. The fort is most known for its vertical 60-meter high rock-cut steps which are almost perpendicular. Despite its tough climb, it attracts many trekkers due to the adventure and panoramic views.
